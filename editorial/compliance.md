# Compliance — RWA, tokenisation, stablecoin content

Applies to every word on screen, in VO, in captions, and in descriptions (CLAUDE.md Section 7). Maintain this file as rules firm up.

- Nothing that reads as investment advice or promises returns, yields, or price movement.
- No "guaranteed", "risk-free", or "secure" as absolutes. Describe controls, not outcomes.
- Regulatory claims cite the regulator and date, or are cut.
- No competitor logos or names without logged approval (decision log in the video's `intake.md`).
- Client names and case studies need written permission, noted in the decision log.
- Every number traces to the claims table in `script.md`, with source and confidence.
- Projections are labelled as projections, never stated as outcomes.
